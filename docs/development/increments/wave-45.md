**`W45-TSAN-ROWS-LOAD-SENSITIVE` (2026-09-26, base `5e9630fe`).** Two "the race must be reported" rows of `compiler/tests/task_route_tsan.rs` failed consecutive integration gates under the test runner's parallel phase and passed alone ([report](../../reports/w45-tsan-rows-load-sensitive/README.md), constraint 16). Measured on the base binary, the file was red in 0 of 10 runs alone and 1/40, 2/64, 13/96 and 42/128 at 8, 16, 24 and 32 concurrent copies. There were two causes, both in the harness. (1) Clang instruments atomics and function entry/exit even in `no_sanitize("thread")` code, so the forcing header's exit barrier, which took and released its spinlock on every poll, gave ThreadSanitizer a new epoch and fresh trace events per iteration. After about 20,000 polls (a partner held about 5 ms) the waiting worker's own history was recycled, and TSan v3 dropped the planted race without output. Raising `history_size` to 7 only moved the cliff to about 10–20 ms. (2) The join-edge-removal stand-in slept 50 ms instead of waiting, so a worker still running after that touched a freed start record, and TSan reported a `heap-use-after-free` instead of a race, or nothing. The barrier now polls a change counter with relaxed loads and takes the lock only when a cohort table changed. The stand-in waits for the worker's completion flag, a relaxed atomic that carries no edge. A worker parked behind a gate only its waiting creator could open is not waited for. `Row::hold` holds one worker for a fixed time, and two new rows hold one for 100 ms: the pre-card barrier fails them 3/3 and the 50 ms stand-in 3/3. After the fix the file is red in 0 of 10, 40, 64, 96 and 128 runs at the same loads, and in 0/64 at 16 copies even with `history_size=0`. No assertion was weakened, no row was ignored, and `EXCLUSIVE` is unchanged. Test-harness only, with no analyser, verdict or Lean change. Retired `TSAN-JOIN-EDGE-ROW-LOAD-SENSITIVE`. Registered `W45-TSAN-QUIET-WAIT-ENFORCED-BEHAVIOURALLY`.
