Card CT07-SLICE-PERMISSIONS — constraint-14 keyword census
==========================================================

Result: ZERO new reserved words, ZERO new tokens, and zero existing files whose
meaning changes.

The card adds a three-argument spelling of an operation that already exists.
`borrow_utf8` has been a CONTEXTUAL clause word since card STRING-CONCAT — it is
skipped by name resolution only where it is immediately followed by `(` — and
the range's two bounds are separated by `,` and closed by `)`, both of which the
lexer has always produced. There is therefore nothing to reserve and no token to
add, and this census exists to check that claim rather than to choose a word.

Scope: every `.qcore`/`.qc` file on disk — 435 at census time, 437 after this
card's two new fixtures.

Reproduce from the repository root:

    find . -path ./.git -prune -o \( -name '*.qcore' -o -name '*.qc' \) -print | wc -l

    grep -rn --include='*.qcore' --include='*.qc' -E 'borrow_utf8\s*\([^)]*,' .

    grep -rho --include='*.qcore' --include='*.qc' \
      -E '\b(lend|slice|range|borrow_utf8|split|part|window|sub|low|high)\b' . \
      | sort | uniq -c | sort -rn

    grep -rn --include='*.qcore' --include='*.qc' \
      -E '\b(lend|range|low|high|part|sub|window)\b' . | grep -v '//'
