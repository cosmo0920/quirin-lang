## Design the prediction assumes

1. **`RETURN-CAST-PLACE-NOT-ESCAPED`** (`legacy_cast_return.rs`): `return (T *) P;`
   escapes `P` like `return P;` for an operand that is neither a declared scalar
   nor a possible descriptor; `(int) fd` of a possible descriptor escapes it; any
   other cast of a possible descriptor, and a non-pointer cast of an operand the
   reader commits to as a pointer, is refused; everything else keeps its
   pre-card answer. No new `LegacyInstruction`. Lean `QCore.CastReturnEscape`.
2. **The refused escape** (`legacy_refused_operands.rs`): at a node whose own
   transfer reported `Unsupported`, the live identities its statement's value
   side hands on (a store's right-hand side or a `return` operand, outside call
   arguments) carry an unresolved effect. Identities passed to a call are **not**
   withdrawn: that accusation rests on a premise about the callee, which is
   class (c). Lean `QCore.RefusedEscapeCustody`.
3. No change to when `report_leaks` runs (intermediate iterates), to any
   contract, or to W4 summaries.
