Every binary expression requires parentheses; there is no implicit precedence
or associativity. Leaves are existing graph atom IDs. `flow(NAME)` explicitly
references another entry in the same declaration. Flow and atom namespaces are
separate. Duplicate entry IDs reject, even with identical definitions. Repeated
atom occurrences are legal shared prerequisites, not duplicate definitions;
dependencies are their sorted union, including every OR branch. References are
resolved over the complete declared entry domain: missing/cyclic entries reject
even when an alternative would otherwise succeed. Limits:256 entries,128 nested
expression/reference steps and4096 expression nodes per declaration.

`A |-> B` is conjunction. `A ||-> B` is the disjunction of complete independent
logical alternatives: one supported alternative suffices, both refuted refute,
and all other cases remain Open. This is not a selected-program-path safety
rule, and does not discharge other feasible paths or a legacy conclusion.
`A !-> B` is A AND NOT B: support needs Supported A and positively Refuted B;
absence/Open never proves negation. A refuted conjunct or supported forbidden
condition refutes this proposed expression. `A ->| B` requires both operands
Supported and produces a discharged terminal flow result; missing/refuted
conditions do not terminate successfully. Terminal is permitted only at the
resolved entry root. Any nested terminal or subsequent continuation, including
through named references, rejects. This is flow termination, not eventual C
program termination or a no-return contract.
