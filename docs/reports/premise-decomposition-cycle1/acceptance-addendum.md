# User-directed concrete mathematics and propagation acceptance

The formal environment is one parent identity, a finite sequence of child
identities, exact ownership/live predicates, child/next/prev maps and a finite
trace of link-writing steps. Each atom maps to a named Lean Prop over that
environment in `QCore.CjsonRepresentationPremises`; atom-map.json owns the
subjects, quantifiers, prerequisites and discharge obligations. Conditional
theorems establish live owned link targets excluding the parent, injective
child indexing, and refutation of writer exclusion by a writing trace step.
They prove mathematical implications; they do not establish imported C
correspondence, the assumptions or the prose/conjunction equivalence.

A synthetic refuted no-writer control must refute only this conjunction in
the exact context. The retained legacy proposition stays separately open;
foreign-context evidence refuses. Machine-readable impact records enumerate
the production consulting rows by actual published hypothesis dependencies
and retain unrelated/context-incompatible records. Propagation is evidence
impact, never Unsafe or a checked C violation. Full runtime records are
compared, and any extra closed-world premise-key metadata is attributed exactly.

A compatibility check also compares `outer_selection::custody_facts` for every
row, including unchanged Unknown rows. The published projection carries tallies
and moved records, so equality of that artifact alone does not establish equality
of closed-world classification. The extra conjunction name must not silently
become a new closed-world fact. If the fresh production corpus exposes that
change, retain the failed migration evidence and publish the decomposition as
a report-owned replayable contract variant while restoring the exact production
contract, inventory and pins. No exception is added to `NOT_CLOSED_WORLD` and no
runtime consumer is weakened to accommodate publication. This contingency is
recorded before the corpus comparison; report-owned publication is a bounded
cycle outcome, with neutral graph attachment tracked as a remaining interface
obligation.
