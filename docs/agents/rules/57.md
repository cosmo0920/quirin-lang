---
rule: 57
topic: gateway
summary: A literal-argument premise stands beside a declaration's synchronous invocations (`resource_callback_invoked`, `resource_callback_invoked_or_default`) and opaque slot invocations (`resource_callback_slot_invoked`) when they are its only retaining clauses; route G's premise rewrite renames an unmet call of such a withheld declaration to an undeclared callee before the callback route reads it, and a met call is decided exactly as rule 13u decides it (a slot invocation as rule 12n decides it), except that under `_or_default` a null pointer constant callback invokes nothing; every other retaining clause beside a premise rejects; a premise-carrying declaration met as a callback candidate (and every candidate under a poisoned premise set) has no contract in force there.
---

57. **A literal-argument premise bounds a synchronous invocation's
    declaration exactly as it bounds a plain one.** Card
    OPENSSL-PEM-CALLBACK-ARM, on the user's decision of 2026-11-18 (remove
    the PEM readers' `x = cb = NULL` restriction; contract specification
    C04.5 "Beside a synchronous invocation", C04.19 placement; grammar G05).
    Loader (`contract_loader.rs`, the retaining branch): a premise stands
    beside retaining clauses only when every one is
    `resource_callback_invoked`; a table, a registration, a registry,
    element or member clause, a retention and every other retaining clause
    beside a premise reject the module (`a literal-argument premise stands
    beside a synchronous invocation only`, or the retention branch's
    refusal). Route G (`legacy_literal_premise::restrict_to_declared_literals`):
    a callback declaration is withheld from the function map, so the rewrite
    treats a retaining declaration as a contracted callee too (the map is
    read directly, not consulted): an unmet premise, a missing actual or a
    poisoned premise set renames the call (`call to … outside its declared
    literal-argument premise`) before the callback route, the retaining
    environment or any other reader sees it, so the callback is never
    decided there. A met call keeps its name and rule 13u decides the
    invocation unchanged: an open set (a formal, a null pointer constant)
    `callback-undecided`, a candidate with no contract in force
    `callback-uncontracted`, a non-identity one `callback-effectful`; the
    premise adds no candidate, contract or mode. **The null default** (user
    decision 2026-11-18, extending the card): `resource_callback_invoked_or_default(F,
    A…)` is the same invocation (schema `CallbackClause::Invoked {
    null_default: true }`, omitted when false) except that a null pointer
    constant at `F` (Clang's `null_leaf`: `NULL`, `0`, a cast of `0`) invokes
    nothing: `Parser::callback_operands` skips the invocation, so the call is
    its custody contract with nothing decided or required. Every other actual,
    including a local or formal that may hold null and a call's result, is
    decided exactly as rule 13u decides it. No body check establishes a null
    default, so a unit defining the declared function withdraws it (rule 13y,
    `callback-withdrawn`; rule 13i classes it `Callback`, uncovered), and rule
    15w neither forwards to nor from it (`stated_invocation`, the own-clause
    match). Measured before the route
    change: a premise-carrying invocation declaration was applied at a
    non-null `x` (`Safe` on the fixture's `reuse_arm`). **Do not** admit a
    premise beside any other callback or retaining clause, read a
    premise-carrying withheld declaration at a call that does not meet it,
    read a null callback as no invocation without the null default, read
    anything but a null pointer constant as null, or forward through a null
    default.

    **Beside a slot invocation** (card OPENSSL-BIO-READER-INVOCATIONS, rule
    57 amended in place; contract specification C04.5 "Beside a slot
    invocation"): the loader also admits `resource_callback_slot_invoked(OBJ,
    TAG.FIELD, A…)` (`CallbackClause::MemberInvoked { slot: true }`) beside a
    premise. It keeps nothing after the call, like the synchronous
    invocation, and route G's rename already covers every retaining
    declaration, so at an unmet call no slot is decided and the call is
    undeclared; at a met call rule 12n decides the slot unchanged (only over
    the run's facts, over the slot's declared registrations, every candidate
    the identity; a key no declaration registers refuses), and the premise
    adds no candidate. A plain member invocation (no `slot`) beside a premise
    still rejects. Used by `PEM_read_bio_X509`, `PEM_read_bio_PrivateKey` and
    `d2i_PKCS12_bio`, which state the 14 BIO slots on their BIO formal with
    `_` actuals. **Do not** admit a plain member invocation, a registration
    or any other clause beside a premise on this ground, or decide a slot at
    a call outside the premise. `QCore.OpensslBioReaders`
    (`a_slot_invocation_stands_beside_a_premise`,
    `an_unmet_premise_decides_no_slot`, `a_met_premise_defers_to_the_slots`,
    `applied_only_over_closed_identity_slots`,
    `the_premise_adds_no_candidate`, the `reader_*` vectors) and
    `QCore.CallbackLiteralPremise` (`the_admission_grows_only_by_slot_invocations`,
    `a_plain_member_invocation_beside_a_premise_rejects`);
    `crates/qcore-contract/tests/callback_literal_premise.rs`,
    `crates/qcore-c-gateway/tests/openssl_undeclared_handshake_callbacks.rs`.
    [Report](../reports/openssl-bio-reader-invocations/README.md).
    **At a callback candidate** (card CALLBACK-CANDIDATE-LITERAL-PREMISE,
    rule 57 amended in place; contract specification C04.5 "At a callback
    candidate"): route G's rewrite bounds the *calls* of a premise-carrying
    declaration, but a callback candidate is a function designator handed
    to an invoker, which chooses its actuals, so the rewrite never sees it.
    The callback route now reads the premise set route G read for the body
    (the parser's `literal_premises`) and gives a candidate whose
    declaration carries a literal-argument premise, and every candidate
    under a poisoned set, no contract in force wherever it is a candidate
    (`LiteralPremises::withdraws_candidate`): `Parser::candidate_withdrawn`
    classifies it `Missing` first in `classified_in`, before its contract,
    its composition (rule 13u amended, `candidate_composition`), its
    hand-off (rule 12m) or its member paths (rule 30) are read, so a
    synchronous, table, member or slot invocation refuses it
    (`callback-uncontracted`, naming it); `Parser::candidate_contract`
    stores no contract for it at a registration (rule 13w) or a constructed
    member registration (rule 12o), so a registry holding it is not
    releasing and an invocation of the member refuses it. The premise is
    not checked against the invocation's actuals: a `_` or a supplied
    object never satisfies it, and a parameter actual is the caller's,
    which the declaration does not bound. Measured before the route change:
    each candidate path applied a premise-carrying identity (or releasing)
    candidate (`Safe` on the fixture's `each_premise`, `each_slot_premise`,
    `list_premise`, `op_premise`). **Do not** apply a premise-carrying
    declaration as a candidate by its contract or its composition, or check
    its premise against an invocation's `_` or supplied actual.
