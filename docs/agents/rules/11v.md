11v. **A resource kind beyond the four has its own carrier, sentinel and
    release protocol; an in-place kind binds its obligation to one automatic
    object, and its initialiser's status is its sentinel.** Card
    CG25-RESOURCE-KINDS (`crates/qcore-c-gateway/src/legacy_in_place_custody.rs`,
    `crates/qcore-contract/src/contract_in_place.rs`, additive items
    `ResourceKind::{DynamicLibrary, Regex, carrier, is_in_place}` and
    `ResourceCarrier` in `qcore-schemas`, `AbstractState::status_witnesses`,
    thin hooks in `legacy_c.rs`: the actual places, the formal-address gate,
    the statement guard, the transfer wrapper, the null-test refinement and
    the CFG check; contract specification §C04.6, §C04.6.1). (1) **Kinds.**
    `dynamic_library` is pointer-carried with failure value NULL and is no
    other kind (`free(h)`, `dlclose(p)` discharge nothing; `dlsym(h, …)`
    after `dlclose(h)` is `use_after_release`). `regex` is **in place**: its
    initialiser (`resource_out(p, nullable)` + kind) creates an obligation in
    the object `p` designates, its finaliser (`resource_released(p,
    nonnull)` + kind) discharges it, and a kinded `resource_borrowed` position
    uses it. (2) **Storage.** An in-place actual is `&NAME` for one automatic,
    non-pointer, non-array object Clang declares once in the function; its
    place is `NAME`. Any other initialiser or finaliser actual refuses; a use
    naming no tracked object keeps the plain borrowed reading. (3) **The
    status is the sentinel.** The initialiser's status must be held by a
    plain, not address-taken name, which witnesses the object until it is
    written (`writes_scalar`); a null test of the witness refines the
    object's binding (zero certifies, nonzero removes); witnesses join by
    intersection. A discarded status, or one consumed by the expression that
    tests it, refuses. (4) **Custody, never data.** A statement mentioning a
    tracked object is admitted only as a contracted call of a declared callee
    whose every mention is a whole `&NAME` actual at an in-place position of
    the object's kind, or as the object's declaration; the formal-address gate
    already refuses `&NAME` at any other position. A call node with an
    in-place initialiser or finaliser lacking its place is refused after the
    CFG is built, so no route drops the effect. **Do not** equate a new kind
    with an existing one, bind an in-place obligation to a member, a global,
    a static or storage reached through a pointer, refine on a status that is
    not the witness, or admit tracked storage on a data route. Premises
    stated, not proved: the libc contracts (`regcomp` returns zero exactly
    when it initialised the object; `dlclose` releases its handle) and
    faithful Clang extraction (trust boundary 28). Measured (corpus
    `66910c10`, system, tail-pause slice, base `b6a3dc42`): corpus 629 / 10 /
    4,195 → **630 / 10 / 4,194** (`3d53a5d0…` → `5ade5aab…`), one Unknown →
    Safe move (`rd_regex_match`: `r = regcomp(&re, …); if (r) return 0; …
    regfree(&re);`), 5 functions changed, every leaf attributed, no
    correction, leak or violation; a possibly-NULL kinded handle returned
    without a contract keeps refusing (`mk_plugin_load_dynamic`,
    `rd_dl_open0`: inferred summaries stay heap, C04.6 rule 7); system and
    slice change no function. Residuals `CG25-IN-PLACE-RESIDUAL-PAIRS`,
    `CG25-KINDED-WRAPPER-RESULTS`, `CG25-DLSYM-SYMBOL-LIFETIME`.
    `QCore.ResourceKinds` (28 theorems, `AuditResourceKinds.lean`; the kind
    table and the protocols bound to Rust and to the libc declarations by
    `kindVectors` and `protocolVectors`); `QCore.KindedCustody.Kind` gains the
    two constructors; [report](../reports/cg25-resource-kinds/README.md).
    (5) **Thread keys** (card CURRENT-CONTEXT-NEW-TRIAGE, D3; user decision
    2026-11-16 "Queue both (Recommended)"; contract specification §C04.6.1;
    qcore-schemas 27.0.0 `ResourceKind::ThreadKey`,
    `ResourceKind::finalised_by_value`). `thread_key` is the second in-place
    kind, finalised by value: libc's `pthread_key_create` (applies only with a
    null destructor, C04.5) initialises `&k`; `pthread_key_delete(k)` takes the
    whole `k` (`in_place_actuals`). A creation into anything but static
    storage refuses (`THREAD_KEY_STORAGE_REFUSAL`): an automatic key would be
    tracked by value, and the card's gate found a statement fallback that
    re-lowered `(void) pthread_getspecific(k)` as a plain borrowed call even
    after `pthread_key_delete(k)`, so no key is tracked. The null destructor is spelled `0` or the C11 null pointer
    constant `(void *) 0` (glibc's `NULL`), admitted at a pointer premise of
    `0` by §C04.5.1 (user decision 2026-11-16 "Admit (void *) 0
    (Recommended)"; `legacy_literal_premise::void_pointer_null_constant`,
    `QCore.NullPointerConstantPremise`). `&G` of an unshadowed **static**-duration object
    (`publication::destinations` minus `thread_local_objects`) binds **no
    frame obligation** and needs no witness: the key is the static holder's
    under the stated premise P-THREAD-KEY-STATIC (created once, no
    destructor, not owed a delete); the call is read with that position
    borrowed (`legacy_classify.rs`). A by-value delete of such a `G` refuses
    (`STATIC_KEY_RELEASE_REFUSAL`), as does a key reached through a pointer,
    a thread-local key and a delete of an untracked key; the getters are not
    kinded, and the guard's own refusal is final (`is_guard_refusal`). The
    same fallback escape for the `regex` kind is closed by (6). **Do not** read
    a thread-local, member or pointer-reached key as static, drop the literal
    destructor premise, or accept a static key's delete without a reading of
    its holder. Measured: see the report (`mk_http_thread_initialize_tls_once`
    predicted `Unknown -> Safe`, corpus and current). `QCore.ThreadKeyStatic`
    (`AuditThreadKeyStatic.lean`), `QCore.KindedCustody.Kind.threadKey`,
    `kindVectors`/`protocolVectors` extended;
    `tests/thread_key_in_place.rs`;
    [report](../reports/current-context-new-triage/README.md).
    (6) **Every mention, whatever the route** (card
    CG25-VOID-CAST-GUARD-ESCAPE, priority soundness; user decision
    2026-11-17 "Yes, launch it."). (4)'s guard reads only a statement's first
    classification. A first classification that was already a refusal skipped
    it, and a later route admitted tracked storage as data. The census found
    25 regex shape runs certified `Safe`, among them `(void) re.used;`,
    `(void) (re.used = 0);` and `(void) (re.used ? 1 : 0);` (statement
    fallbacks 10s and 10z), `t = k_regexec(&re, s) + (int) re.used;`,
    `if`/`while`/`do`/`switch` on `re.used`, and `return (int) re.used;`.
    So, over the finished CFG (after `verify_cfg` and every later pass), the
    body's token stream (the one the parser reads) is read once
    (`InPlaceNames::body_mentions`, `verify_mentions`). Every variable
    mention of a tracked object must be one of three things: its declarator
    (the guard's `declares_only` over the enclosing statement, not after a
    statement keyword); the operand of `sizeof NAME` or `sizeof ( NAME )`
    (not evaluated, C11 6.5.3.4p2; a tracked object is never an array); or a
    **custody actual**. A custody actual is a whole `&NAME` actual (or `NAME`
    at a by-value finaliser or use of a by-value kind) of a direct call to a
    declared callee with a contract in force, at a position of the object's
    kind. Every custody actual must also be carried by at least as many call
    nodes of that callee as the body spells it, each holding the object's
    place at that position. Otherwise a refusal node is added before the
    entry, so it lies on every path and replaces no lowering. Names are read
    off the tokens, not off the nodes, so a by-value scalar actual is seen:
    D3's CFG-level check missed that case. Widening (4)'s guard to every
    refusal is also not needed, which is what broke `rd_regex_match`. Every
    correctly admitted use stays admitted, with its place: kinded calls
    plain, assigned, compared (10y), cast, in a statement expression (11g),
    in a condition, a declaration or a return, and `sizeof`. No published
    Safe rested on an escape: `rd_regex_match`, the only tracked object on
    any route or baseline, names `re` only in custody actuals. **Do not**
    admit a mention because some route lowered it, or relax the carried
    count to "some node". `QCore.InPlaceMentionGuard`
    (`AuditInPlaceMentionGuard.lean`; every admitted lowering, from any
    route, passed the guard; `shapeAdmissions` bound to
    `tests/in_place_mention_guard.rs`);
    [report](../reports/cg25-void-cast-guard-escape/README.md).
