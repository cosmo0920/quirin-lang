49. **Groups that test the same guard are kept or skipped together; the
    definition census may say so, and nothing more.** Card
    TAIL-FILE-DEFINITION-CENSUS-INCOMPLETE
    (`crates/qcore-c-gateway/src/legacy_guard_correlation.rs`, one call in
    `legacy_enumeration.rs`'s `raw_half`, and `tokenize_with_directives` in
    `legacy_tokens.rs`). It amends unit E01-RAW-COND's census refusal, not
    its raw scan: `legacy_c_source_scan` and every other reader of
    `function_names` are unchanged. (1) **When.** Only when the raw scan's
    sole ground is `ArmDepthDisagreement`; an unstructured group, a missing
    expansion or any other ground keeps the refusal as before. (2) **The
    reading.** One raw scan per *world*, a scan state with assumptions
    `NAME is (not) defined`. A *guard group* — opened by exactly `#ifdef
    NAME` or `#ifndef NAME` (white space, splices and one trailing comment
    only), with at most an `#else` and no `#elif` — is read under the
    world's assumption, splitting the world when it has none; the arm a
    world does not keep is skipped by that world. Every other group is read
    as E01-RAW-COND reads it, each arm from the entry depth, and a world in
    which its arms leave different depths refuses the whole reading. Every
    other directive (`#define`, `#undef`, `#include`, `#pragma`, …) and every
    `_Pragma`/`__pragma` operator in a world's kept text clears that world's
    assumptions. Worlds with equal scan states merge, keeping only shared
    assumptions; more than 64 worlds refuse, and so does any directive that
    spells `_Pragma`, `__pragma`, `push_macro` or `pop_macro` (a macro
    carrying one changes the macro table where it is invoked, an ordinary
    identifier to this reading). No controlling expression is evaluated; the
    premise is E01-RAW-COND's (the raw half reads written text, not
    expansions, and the expansion half stays authoritative for the
    configuration compiled). (3) **What it claims.** The worlds cover every valuation of
    every guard in every epoch between directives, so every definition any
    configuration keeps at file scope, guarded-out arms included, is reached
    at file scope by some world and collected; a merged world may follow a
    configuration no preprocessor reaches, which only adds a name. The
    collected names are unioned with the raw scan's (a superset of them) and
    the refusal is dropped, so the census is complete; otherwise the census
    stays refused exactly as before. **Do not** read an `#if` expression, an
    `#elif` chain or a guard through a macro as a guard group, or decide a
    disagreement by selecting an arm. Measured: Fluent Bit 90978ac6's
    `plugins/in_tail/tail_file.c` (`tag_compose`, two `#ifdef
    FLB_HAVE_REGEX` groups opening and closing one `else {`) is decided and
    the tail-pause slice's import phase runs; no system or corpus unit has
    the refusal. `QCore.GuardCorrelatedCensus` (`the_measured_shape_is_sound`,
    `the_domain_is_sound` over every stream of length at most four,
    `a_split_covers_every_value`, `a_merge_only_weakens`,
    `the_domain_checksum_is_pinned`, …), bound to
    `crates/qcore-c-gateway/tests/guard_correlated_census.rs`;
    [report](../reports/tail-file-definition-census-incomplete/README.md).
