6. **C02-P-ALIAS-LEAK-ORDER:** `report_leaks` on intermediate iterates is
   scheduling-sensitive; a pre-pass keeps it scheduling-neutral with
   `AbstractState` byte-identical. Recorded, not repaired — do not disturb it
   incidentally. `remaining-c02-p-alias`, `remaining-borrow-liveness`,
   `remaining-hygiene-14`.
<!-- preserved:constraint-6:end -->

Rule 6 is amended by [rule 74c](#constraint-74c): a current-generation leak
report made on an iterate is published only if the settled exit does not mark
its identity unresolved beside an `Unknown` leaf.

Rule 6 is amended by [rule 84](#constraint-84) (card
SETTLED-LEAK-OTHER-REFUSAL-SCHEDULE): a leak whose settled exit marks the
identity by any refusal beside an `Unknown` leaf is decided from the settled
states alone, whatever iterate claimed it.

Rule 6 is amended by [rule 84](#constraint-84) (card
SETTLED-LEAK-MARK-WITHOUT-UNKNOWN-LEAF): the same holds beside a `Pending`
leaf (a call held for a human act); a mark beside no blocking leaf is still
rule 74c's.
