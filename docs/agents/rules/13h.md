13h. **The dependency record is evidence: no verdict reads it, and removing
    it restores every report byte for byte.** Card
    VERDICT-CONTRACT-DEPENDENCIES: the row field, the unit field and the
    sidecar's `contract_dependencies` section (each route's sidecar; the
    composer folds it from the rows like the refutation section, under the
    `contract_premises` the current route records before its units). The
    premise clause `requires resource_premise(NAME, "STATEMENT");`
    (contract specification C04.18) changes no mode and is covered by the
    pin digest (control: without the re-pin both premised declarations are
    `contract-changed`, not in force). Measured on the base's inputs plus the
    premise: corpus report projection byte-identical to BEFORE (`36fd1649…`);
    system and slice verdict rows identical (`c000abd8…` / `0cd51920…` raw
    BEFORE; their only other differences are the contract-source digests the
    current route's `compile_configuration` names), and the system run
    before the premise (`a3`) byte-identical in full; parity multisets
    identical on all three. Card LIBRDKAFKA-PLUGIN-PREMISE-REMAINDER: the
    clause's shape is the contract format's, refused at parse in every
    module. Bounds v1 admits it only beside a scoped member form (rule 14x).
    A Quirin model contract may state it (`contract_loader::module_premises`).
    **Amended by card OUTER-CONTRACT** (rule 31; USER DECISION 2026-10-17,
    D3 "Both from one run"): the runtime projection is the **one reader** of
    the dependency record that publishes a verdict. It reads the record after
    every static verdict is written, publishes its verdicts in its own
    document (`QCORE_OUTER_PROJECTION`) beside the unchanged static ones, and
    only lowers (the join with the static verdict); no static verdict reads
    the record, and the record gains the run-set and stated member keys
    (`run_set_members`, `stated_members`) of the declarations a row rests on.
    `QCore.ContractDependencies`
    (`publishing_the_set_changes_no_verdict`,
    `two_sets_publish_the_same_verdict_row`); `QCore.OuterContract`
    (`static_projection_is_identity`, `runtime_never_promotes`).
    **Amended by card SP1-SYMBOLIC-OPENSSL-PREMISES** (contract
    specification C04.18.9; USER DECISION 2026-11-20): the statement may be
    a formula, `requires resource_premise(NAME, FORMULA);`, over the closed,
    sorted vocabulary C04.18.9 registers (`qcore_frontend::premise_formula`,
    the one reader the format and the loader share). A malformed formula, an
    unknown term or constant, an ill-sorted application, an unbound or
    shadowing variable, and a string that is itself a formula reject the
    whole module; the premise is never dropped. Its identity, and what
    every reader of the record prints, is the name plus the canonical
    printing (set difference is the one-byte lexer token `\`, refused
    outside a formula; the hypothesis graph admits a backslash only in a
    generated legacy atom whose statement is exactly such a canonical
    printing). It is still read by no verdict: a formula that reads makes
    nothing `Safe`. `quirin-provider-openssl` states all 53 of its premises
    as formulas and its checker refuses a string statement
    (`prose_premise`). **Do not** drop or weaken a premise whose formula
    does not read, read a premise's formula as a discharged fact, or admit
    a term outside the registered vocabulary without its specification row
    and Lean model. `QCore.PremiseFormula` (`check_denotes`, `wf_denotes`,
    `decision_vectors_agree`, `openssl_formulas_wf`,
    `unstated_entails_ex_data`, `ex_data_does_not_entail_unstated`, …);
    [report](../reports/sp1-symbolic-openssl-premises/README.md).
    **Amended by card SP2-SYMBOLIC-LIBRDKAFKA-PREMISES** (C04.18.9's
    librdkafka block): `qcore-provider-librdkafka` states all 220 of its
    premises as formulas (12 names, 16 statements) over 75 appended terms,
    the falsum `#false` among them (a negation is `P -> #false`); no sort,
    operator or typing rule is added. The crate's own test refuses a string

    check), and a binding manifest that pins a premised declaration's graph
    is refreshed with the graph digest its only change. The source operator
    gate reads a formula's `|` and `&&` as the formula's own. **Do not**
    match a premise by a string the formula replaced, or carry a frozen
    checkpoint across the conversion except through the card's recorded,
    hash-checked restoration (`tests/support/sp2_premise_checkpoint.rs`).
    `QCore.PremiseFormula.Librdkafka` (`librdkafka_formulas_wf`,

    **Amended by card SP5-GLOBAL-PROSE-PREMISE-BAN** (contract
    specification C04.18; USER DECISION 2026-11-20): the string statement
    `resource_premise(NAME, "STATEMENT")` is retired. A statement that is or
    holds a string literal rejects the whole module with `QF001` (namespace
    `QF***`, [diagnostics](diagnostics.md#premise-formula-namespace-qf)), in
    every vocabulary and on every route, since the parser and every loader
    read a premise through `qcore_frontend::premise_formula::premise_clause`,
    whose result holds no string statement; nothing drops the premise. This
    is the load-time ban for every provider crate, shipped or user, beside
    the per-crate checks SP1-SP4 added (kept). C04.21's reserved
    entry-snapshot premise states the formula
    `quant all #m in #condition_reads: #m in #entry_stable` (C04.18.9's SP5
    block) in place of its sentence, matched exactly as before. A frozen
    text that states a string premise is read only by the test-side legacy
    reader `tests/support/legacy_prose_premise.rs`, which writes each
    statement card SP2-SYMBOLIC-LIBRDKAFKA-PREMISES recorded as its formula
    and is confined to test code. **Do not** add a flag, feature or entry
    point that admits a string statement, or read a frozen string premise
    except through that reader. `QCore.PremiseFormula`
    (`string_is_not_a_premise`, `formula_premise_iff_wf`, `accepted_is_wf`,
    `accepted_is_a_formula`, `accepted_denotes`, `clause_vectors_agree`);
    [report](../reports/sp5-global-prose-premise-ban/README.md).

    specification C04.18.1, C04.18.7, C04.18.9's SP6 block; USER DECISION
    2026-11-20; user 2026-11-22: "We need to convert to be symbolized atoms

    "STATEMENT")` is retired too. A clause headed `resource_hypothesis`,
    under any introducer, rejects the whole module with `QF002` at parse

    **Amended by card SP6-GUARDED-SYMBOL-PROSE** (contract specification
    C04.18.5, C04.18.6, C04.18.9's SP7 block; same user decision; the
    user's decisions of 2026-11-23):

    * The string forms of the guarded source heads are retired. A string in
      `resource_guarded_symbol_v1` or `resource_guarded_decomposition_v1`,
      under any introducer, rejects the whole module with `QF003` at parse
      (`qcore_frontend::guarded_decomposition::parse_source_clause`), so on
      every route.
    * A guarded binding's proposition must be the canonical printing of a
      C04.18.9 typed-rendering formula. The lowering (standalone `replay`
      included) and the bridge `render` refuse anything else with `QF003`.
    * A symbol states an unquoted formula. A document states its unquoted
      clauses (`&&` for `∧`), and the readable document is rendered from
      them unchanged.
    * The eight English symbols of `cJSON_DetachItemViaPointer` are
      formulas over registered and SP7 terms, among them

      assumptions that no model, source check or assignment rule derives.
      The refutation entails non-ownership, and the `condition` role still
      requires Supported invalidity and positively Refuted admission.
    * Guarded symbols are still read by no verdict.

    **Do not** admit a string or non-formula guarded proposition, derive
    `#source_corresponds` or `#caller_refutes_membership` from any fact, or
    read the frozen string forms except through the legacy reader.
    `QCore.CjsonGuardedSymbols` (`guarded_symbols_are_the_definitions`,
