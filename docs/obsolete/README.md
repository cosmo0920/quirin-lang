# Superseded QCore specifications

Current requirements are in [revision 0.3](../QCORE_SPEC_r0_3.md), its
[contract specification](../QCORE_CONTRACT_SPEC_r0_3.md) and
[implemented grammars](../QCORE_IMPLEMENTED_GRAMMARS_r0_3.md).

This directory preserves revision 0.1, the revision 0.2 concurrency amendment,
the consolidated revision 0.2 specification, its changelog and its historical
validation narrative. Their original implementation claims and old references
are records of those revisions, not statements of current support. Unchanged
older design requirements remain target requirements under v0.3's precedence
rule; their presence here does not constitute implementation evidence.

- [Sequential revision 0.1](qcore_quantitative_types_spec.md)
- [Concurrency amendment](concurrency_revision.md)
- [Consolidated revision 0.2](QCORE_SPEC_r0_2.md)
- [Revision 0.2 changelog](CHANGELOG_r0_2.md)
- [Historical design validation](VALIDATION_r0_2.md)

Dated audit reports and immutable source/trace metadata elsewhere retain their
original paths and claims. The original supplied v0.3 review is archived separately
under `../reviews/r0_3/supplied`; it is provenance, not the current acceptance ledger.
